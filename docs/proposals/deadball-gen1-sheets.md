# The First Generation, man by man

**Status: design, in consultation (opened 2026-11-14).** Design sheets for the 32 First Generation players in [deadball-gen1.js](../../client/shared/deadball-gen1.js), for phase 5 of [deadball-players-v2.md](deadball-players-v2.md). Ratings, positions and handedness are the roster's own and don't change here. Twelve men carry the new names that proposal settled.

Each sheet gives:

- **Read:** how you'd know him at TV distance, from his silhouette alone.
- **Changed:** what's been done to his body, and where it fits the game's own catalog.
- **Moves:** what his body does to the way he plays.
- **Routine:** his signature before a pitch, or on the mound.
- **Flip:** his temperament and his own bat flip (batters only, because the league has a DH). No two men share a flip. His order flavours it, and his temperament decides when he does it: a showman on any big hit or walk, a hothead on a home run and in anger on a strikeout, an old pro or a stoic on a home run only, and only small.
- **Kit:** how he wears the uniform.
- **Face:** what the close-up shows.
- **Effects:** light, sound and particles.

## The four orders' rules

These come from the lore and bind every sheet.

**Ascendants: bought, polished, Halcyon's.** An Ascendant replaces what he was born with ([systems-mastery.md](../systems-mastery.md)), and he buys the replacement from Halcyon. His parts come from the augment catalog (`content/augments/`), and they look expensive: white ceramic, frosted glass, gilt inlay, hairline seams, a maker's hallmark and serial number engraved small. Nothing shows wear, because a scuff goes back for service. Light shows only at the seams, pale blue. A club with an Ascendant on it carries Halcyon service drones as batboys: small white craft that wait on the dugout rail. Every Ascendant flip ends with a drone taking the bat, because an Ascendant doesn't pick up after himself. Drones never fly while the ball's live.

**The Long Watch: nothing added.** They master the body they were issued, so nothing is fitted or grown. What marks them is condition: hands taped instead of gloved, old training scars, an ochre bar down the brow, wraps, beads and cords. Their skill shows in how little they move. The Crows draft both of theirs first, so most of these men wear Crows black.

**Wildblood: grown, and chosen.** Each change was asked for (a Gift) or arrived instead (a Lesson), and both are worn proudly ([lore-wildblood.md](../lore-wildblood.md)). So every Wildblood man has a Gift that helps him play and a Lesson that doesn't. Nothing machine shows on them, ever. Their changes come from the mutation catalog (`content/mutations/`) through the figure's `MUTATION_LOOK` and grown bones, and the kit is cut and slit to fit what grew. Ash and blood marks from the ritual stay on their skin.

**The Null: analog, open, maintained.** Their limbs are cable and spring, with cover plates that open, and none of it carries a radio, because "anything with a radio in it is a sensor pointed at you" ([story.md](../story.md#the-nulls-answer)). City chrome costs a Null standing, so where a man once had it there's a scarred socket stopped with solder. Every prosthesis has a servicing card tied to it. Nothing lights up and nothing glows. Their effect goes the other way: now and then, on a close shot or a replay of a Null man and never during a pitch, the broadcast picture tears, rolls or drops a few frames, and the booth apologises for technical difficulties. It's rare, so it stays a surprise. Reduced motion and the accessibility settings turn it off.

## Ascendants

### Aurel Vance, The Gilt Arm

P · throws R · arm 10

- **Read:** a white-and-gold right arm, bare from the shoulder, on an otherwise ordinary pitcher.
- **Changed:** his whole right arm, shoulder to fingertips, is Halcyon's top line: Myomer Bundles under porcelain plate, with gilt filigree inlaid along the seams. The shoulder mount sits proud of the jersey like a pauldron. He also has the Ocular Array, so his eyes are grey optics with a fine bright ring. A ceramic spine channel shows as a raised ridge down his back through the jersey.
- **Moves:** over the top at about 80°, high release, long extension. The arm does everything without waste, while his flesh side, the left leg and hip, visibly works harder. After the release the arm settles back to centre in three small clicks.
- **Routine:** between pitches he takes a white cloth from his back pocket and wipes the arm down. He keeps the product clean.
- **Kit:** right sleeve cut away at the shoulder. Cap dead straight.
- **Face:** composed and faintly amused. He doesn't sweat.
- **Effects:** the seams brighten through the wind-up, and a thin shimmer of heat runs along the arm at release. A clean servo whir. No sparks.

### Cassian Wren, Halo

SS · R/R · contact 7, speed 8, arm 7 (was Solace Wren)

- **Read:** a chrome half-ring behind his neck, standing up above the collar.
- **Changed:** the Halcyon Halo Collar, which the Curators wear and which is issued rather than sold, so he's somebody's protégé. Seams run from the collar into the skin of his neck. He also has the Ocular Array.
- **Moves:** the collar reads the room half a second early. On a ball in play its weave ripples toward where the ball is going before he moves, and his first step is always right.
- **Routine:** in the field he settles with his hands on his knees, then rises a beat before the pitch, already leaning.
- **Flip:** stoic. Lets go behind him without looking, and a drone takes it before it touches the dirt. He never checks; the collar told him it would be there.
- **Kit:** neat, high socks, the collar outside the jersey.
- **Face:** calm, a little bored.
- **Effects:** the ripple in the collar, a faint blue along the seams at his neck.

### Kasimir Oduya, Chrome Clipper

1B · R/R · power 10, speed 1 · 6'10"

- **Read:** the biggest man on the field, square as a door, chest like a boiler.
- **Changed:** the Rad-sealed Chassis, a ceramic-and-lead shell over his whole core, so his torso is rigid and enormously heavy. Pauldrons cap both shoulders. A ceramic jaw plate runs ear to ear.
- **Moves:** the chassis barely twists, so his swing is all hips and arms round a torso that doesn't bend, unlike anyone else's. He runs like furniture being moved. Every step puts a puff of dust off the dirt.
- **Routine:** he taps the plate once with the bat, hard enough to hear, and doesn't move again.
- **Flip:** showman. The launch, end over end and high, and a drone climbs to catch it at the top of its arc.
- **Kit:** a jersey tailored over the shell, with buttons strained at the chest.
- **Face:** a big easy grin above the jaw plate.
- **Effects:** a deep thud on each step, and a low hum from the chassis.

### Aldous Lisle, Second Skin

CF · L/L · speed 10 · 5'10" (was Petra Lisle)

- **Read:** a slight, pale, hairless man who's too smooth, like a statue running.
- **Changed:** his skin is the Subdermal Weave, grown through all of it, so it's synthetic dermis with no pores and no hair, and a faint hexagonal sheen when the light rakes across it. He has no eyebrows. Sprint Actuators in both calves show as smooth fins at the back of his legs.
- **Moves:** the actuators give him a perfectly even stride. His cadence never changes from first base to home, which looks wrong on purpose.
- **Routine:** he bounces on his toes twice and settles, exactly the same every time, to the frame.
- **Flip:** stoic. Drops it mid-stride out of the box, and a drone swoops low and clamps it off the dirt in step with him.
- **Kit:** socks cut away at the calf to free the fins.
- **Face:** no lines. When he smiles, nothing else on his face moves.
- **Effects:** lines along the calves light as he sprints, and a high, quiet servo whine.

### Dorian Saltmarsh, The Ledger

C · R/R · arm 8 · thick

- **Read:** a catcher with a crown of fine pins at the back of his skull, seen when the mask comes up.
- **Changed:** the Neural Co-processor, a ring of sensor pins round the back of the head, and the Cortical Backup. He's died and come back, and can afford to. The Sternal Power Cell shows as a soft glow in the gap of his chest protector.
- **Moves:** he keeps a book on every hitter. His signs come fast and certain.
- **Routine:** before every sign he touches two fingers to his temple. Between innings he talks to the umpire constantly.
- **Flip:** old pro. Holds the bat out sideways at arm's length, and a drone takes it from his hand the way a waiter takes a coat.
- **Kit:** gear immaculate, the chest protector in white with gilt trim.
- **Face:** heavy-lidded, mouth always going.
- **Effects:** the chest glow, and the crown's pins lighting in sequence as he calls the pitch.

### Anselm Marrow, The Gilt Edge

3B · R/R · contact 8, power 7 (was Ilse Marrow, Gilt Lady)

- **Read:** a gold jaw, and five gilt-trimmed drones of his own on the dugout rail.
- **Changed:** old Halcyon money. His jaw is replaced in gilt, with gold seams running down his neck. He has five attendant drones besides the club's.
- **Moves:** a clean, expensive-looking swing, level and patient.
- **Routine:** his drones wait on the rail while the ball's live, and fly out between innings and at pitching changes to fan him, towel him and hold a mirror.
- **Flip:** showman. The spin flip, and his own five catch it and pass it round a ring between them before they carry it to the rack.

### Benedikt Croft, Glasshand

P · throws R · arm 9

- **Read:** a hand you can see through.
- **Changed:** his right hand is clear glass over a fine mechanism of white tendons and bright joints. In the dugout he wears a white stole, like a Curator's, and takes it off to pitch.
- **Moves:** a three-quarter finesse pitcher, slow tempo, everything off command.
- **Routine:** before each pitch he holds the ball up and looks at it through his own fingers.
- **Kit:** the stole on the bench, folded over the rail.
- **Face:** devout and quiet.
- **Effects:** the grip close-up is his showpiece. You can see the grip through the glass even when the ball is in the glove, so his tell always shows, and the booth knows it.

### Julian Castellane, The Ascension

DH · S/R · contact 10, arm 1 (was Yuna Castellane)

- **Read:** the most replaced man in the league, white plate from crown to heel, with ordinary flesh arms.
- **Changed:** nearly everything Halcyon sells. The Seraph Lattice, "the grade The First wears", runs from throat to hip, with the Ocular Array, the crown of pins and anchoring feet. He's kept his own arms. He's said why in no interview (which is why he can't throw).
- **Moves:** the anchors lock his feet into the box with an audible click and release with a hiss. He switch-hits, and his left and right swings are exact mirror images, which no human's are.
- **Routine:** steps in, locks, and holds still. No practice swings.
- **Flip:** stoic. A drone docks to the knob during his follow-through, and he simply opens his hands and walks off.
- **Kit:** long sleeves to the wrist, buttoned, to keep the flesh arms covered.
- **Face:** serene. His optics track the pitcher's hand without his head moving.
- **Effects:** the lattice's light running down his torso as he swings, and the lock and hiss of the feet.

## Decided in consultation (2026-11-14)

- The Null break-up happens rarely, on close shots and replays only, never during a pitch.
- Anselm Marrow's drones stay on the dugout rail during play and come out between innings.
- Kester Thorn's scuffs are in the sim, small, with a very small chance of an ejection.
- Every batter has his own flip, flavoured by his order. Every Ascendant flip ends with a Halcyon drone taking the bat.
