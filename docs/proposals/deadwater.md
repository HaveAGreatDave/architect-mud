# Deadwater — the Null's works

> **Status: Phases 1–2 BUILT (2026-08-12). Jamming (Phase 3) and the confrontation (Phase 4) are
> design.** The region, the works, both overland crossings, the freight economy, seven NPCs and the
> Null's promotion to a canonical order all ship. The `jam` verb, `item_null_jammer`, chrome
> suppression and the march east do not exist yet, and the `flags.emp_shadow` stamped on all 4,836
> tiles is read by nothing today.

*Deadwater* is the still, unmoving water in a ship's wake. The name sits in the Coldwater naming
family so it reads as the same map's cartography, and the "dead" does the creed's work without ever
explaining itself. Region id `region_deadwater`; zone ids `zone_dw_<x>_<y>`, matching the grid
exactly (the Reach's legacy `x-40 / y+972` offset is a trap and is not repeated here).

---

## The rules this region is written under

### 1. The war is real; the reason is unfalsifiable

The Null intend to cross the entire world and switch the Architect off. **That march is real, costed,
and staffed** — there is a chalk column on a wall with a number at the bottom of it, and the number
is currently fifty-three.

**Exactly one person believes the world is a simulation he can delete himself from.** He is Imre
Cobbald, he cannot support the belief, and he says so before the player can:

> *"None. I have looked for eleven years and I have found nothing that is not also explained by the
> world simply being the world. And I know that any evidence I did find, the idea would eat."*

The strongest refutation in the region is in **Selke Machin's** mouth, and she is going east anyway,
because her objection to the Architect never depended on the metaphysics:

> *"I do not care whether it is real. I care that nobody chose it."*

Nothing in the world confirms or denies the simulation, and **nothing ever may**. The five shipped
`content/npc_banter_threads/bt_sim_debate*.json` threads — in which the hypothesis is raised and the
skeptic wins — stay valid and become foreshadowing rather than contradiction. **No quest may ever
reward a player for working out which of them is right, because there is no fact of the matter to
work out.** One line breaking this collapses the faction into a plot twist.

Note who *refuses* to supply the reason: Maud Threlfall, who runs the works and has costed the whole
expedition, answers "why" with *"Reasons are not on the board. Hours are on the board."* The
authority figure endorses the march without ever endorsing the why. That is what keeps it open.

### 2. The analog is stated only as maintenance

Nobody delivers a manifesto about dependence. They talk about a chain that wants dressing flat, a
tolerance, a tray of bearings in order, and an arm that needs cleaning out every eight days because
the grit gets in.

Selke's forearm is in a vice with the cover plate off when you meet her, and what she is talking
about is the **water rota**, which she thinks is unfair. She never explains the arm. The closest
anybody comes to a creed is Perrin Ockley on city chrome:

> *"Mine are worse. Mine open."*

### 3. Nobody remarks on the ion storm

An ion storm does nothing here, and **not one line of content observes it** — no ambient beat, no NPC
remark, no forecast copy. People do not remark on the absence of a thing that has never happened to
them. The absence of the line *is* the line.

### 4. Everything here is legible (the difference from the Thornwarren)

The Scarletwastes runs on *terror outside, domestic inside, nothing remarks on it*. Repeating that
shape would make two regions read as one idea twice.

Deadwater's disquiet is the opposite: **every mechanism has its cover off.** Inspection covers stand
against their housings. The stores is unlocked and indexed on paper in duplicate. The schoolroom
clock has its case off so the children can watch the escapement let go. Nothing is hidden from you
at all. These people are not savage. They are **calm**, and they are calm about something enormous.

### Ash outside, gravel inside the works

The open region is **`ash`** — burnt grey waste that takes a print and keeps it. The works platform is
**`gravel`**, graded hardstanding these people laid and go on maintaining.

**That split is rule 4 expressed in terrain:** the ground changes underfoot at exactly the line where
somebody started looking after it. Two coherent zones is not the same thing as scattering terrain
about; inside each one it stays a flat canvas for the Studio, exactly as the Scarletwastes ships
uniform `redrock`. Do not "improve" it by speckling terrain in code.

`ash` also carries its **own flight biome** rather than falling into generic `badlands`
([plugins/flight/biomes.js:35](../../plugins/flight/biomes.js)), so the region reads as distinct from
the air for free.

One thing no line of content anywhere says: **what burned, or when.** Ash with an explanation
attached is a backstory. Ash without one is a place.

> **This is the third unpainted canvas.** The Scarletwastes is still awaiting its Studio pass, the
> Reach's 56 new southern rows are uniform `scrub`, and this adds 4,391 tiles of grey. That is real
> and growing content debt, recorded here rather than discovered later.

### The dark region, and how it is done safely

**One `power_zones` row per tile, with `generator_id: null`.**

`simulatePowerNetwork` Phase 6 ([environment.js:1598](../../server/engine/environment.js)) sweeps
`zonesByGen.get('__orphan__')` and writes `offline` **every cycle**, so an orphan row is dark with no
new engine code and no way for a dev-panel action to light it. This is the same shape the sewer layer
uses (`content/power_zones/zone_under_*.json`, *"city_grid, 0 kW → pitch dark"*).

Precisely: dark from the **first power cycle**, not from the first millisecond. `power_zones.status`
is in `excludeColumns` in [content-registry.js:238](../../server/models/content-registry.js)
(*"recomputed every power cycle"*), so it is runtime state and cannot be authored — a freshly imported
row carries the schema default `powered` until the sim first runs and Phase 6 converges it. That
window is structural rather than a defect here, and The Under has lived in it since it shipped.

The rows must **exist**, and this is the non-obvious part. A *missing* row is read as unpowered by
`getZonePowerStatus`, lights, warmth and synthesis — but as **powered** by
[plugins/atm/index.js:197](../../plugins/atm/index.js) and as **live** by
[plugins/broadcast/index.js:4065](../../plugins/broadcast/index.js). Leaving the rows out would have
given a deliberately dark region working cash machines in the dark.

There is also deliberately **no `content/generators/` row** for this region. The Scarletwastes has
one; Deadwater not having one is its argument made in schema rather than in prose.

### The dam is not a chokepoint, and nothing pretends it is

The dam is building **mass** (`building_type` gates solidity in `groundObstructionAt` — without it a
vehicle drives straight through, because exits stop walkers and nothing else). It claims **no map
code**: `building_type` only, no `is_building` and no `building_name`, because sixteen tiles reaching
for the same two letters is the marker-namespace exhaustion Terminus hit at *thirteen*, and a barrier
is not a landmark.

But the lake is only ten tiles deep in a region fifty-two tall, so **a walker who wants the north
shore can simply go round.** The Gallery is the short way, not the only way. The two shoulders (the
Spillway and the Gauge House) are walkable, because they are places you stand in rather than stone
you walk into — turning them into mass gave them authored prose no player could ever reach, which is
exactly what happened on the first run.

### Access: two ways in, so the southwest is a triangle

All of it is table edits in [plugins/voidwalking/index.js](../../plugins/voidwalking/index.js)
`VOIDS`; crossing generation, hard nodes, encounters and teardown are generic over the table.

| From | To | `dir` | Lands at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coldwater | Deadwater | `west` | `zone_dw_812_955` (the Roadhead) |
| Deadwater | Coldwater | `north` | `zone_district_870_947` |
| Deadwater | The Reach | `east` | `zone_the_reach_863_1956` |
| The Reach | Deadwater | `west` | `zone_dw_818_988` (the Eastern Ruts) |

Three things to know before touching this:

1. **Coldwater's fork is now FULL at three limbs.** `dir: 'west'` was not a preference, it was the
   last cardinal left (`reach` holds south, `exodus` holds east, and north is the basin — a water
   tile has no rim in any direction, so there was never a fourth). A further destination off
   Coldwater needs a design change, not another row in the array.
2. **Deadwater→Coldwater is the one dest in the table that is not the mirror of its outbound leg.**
   Coldwater lies entirely north of Deadwater *and* entirely east of it, so both readings are true,
   and `east` was already spoken for by the Reach. `RIM_DELTA` is cardinal-only; there are no
   diagonals to escape into.
3. **The Reach's `trunk` rose 1 → 2**, which is the only shipped crossing this work changed shape.
   Its Coldwater limb keeps `length: 8`, so the corridor is the same distance and the tank maths in
   `flight-model.js` still holds — only the trunk/limb split moved. A trunk of 2 stays detour-free
   (`trunkLen >= 3`), which is right: the gamble belongs on the way out.

### Freight: one region, two live routes

[plugins/trucking/market.js](../../plugins/trucking/market.js) gains
`region_deadwater: { scrap: 0.74, water: 0.82, protein: 1.24, chems: 1.32, parts: 0.70, medical: 1.30 }`,
read straight off the geography. It **makes** parts and **strips** scrap, so both go out cheap; it is
sitting on the head of the Basin's water, so water is cheap too; nothing grows on graded gravel and
nobody refines anything, so protein, chems and medical all come in dear.

The better half of that was not designed in: **the two corridors trade different boxes.** Coldwater
wants scrap (1.28) and sells medical and protein cheap, so that run is salvage out and food and
medicine back. The Reach wants parts (1.30) and sells chems cheap (0.78), so *that* run is machined
parts out and drums back. Neither route is the other one with the labels changed.

`REGION_LABEL` in [plugins/trucking/index.js](../../plugins/trucking/index.js) gains
`region_deadwater: 'Deadwater'` — without it `regionLabel` falls through to the raw id on a freight
board. The Roadhead carries `flags.truck_depot` **and `flags.truck_fuel`**; without a pump the
Terminus stranding bug recurs.

Seven, faction `ideology_null`, names checked against all 198 existing NPCs for shared given names
and surnames. No em dashes anywhere in their prose.

| NPC | Where | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| **Maud Threlfall** | The Tally | Runs the works. Costs the march, refuses to supply the reason |
| **Imre Cobbald** | The Reckoning | The believer, and his own best refutation |
| **Selke Machin** | The Bench | The counter-argument, going east anyway. The set-piece tile |
| **Perrin Ockley** | The Surgery | Analog limbs, and a servicing card tied to every wrist |
| **Hobbe Padgett** | The Stores | Nothing locked, everything indexed, twice |
| **Yestin Grieve** | The Grey Yard | The hand-cranked jammer, and the shelf under the cloth |
| **Wenna Denholm** | The Schoolroom | The open clock, and what the march costs the people staying |

**Banter is scoped to `personality: 'labourer'` and written with no proper nouns**, and both halves
matter. A thread with `personality: null` lands in the **generic** pool
([npc-banter.js](../../server/engine/npc-banter.js)), so any NPC anywhere can run it — which is why
the Thornwarren's threads, naming Ossa and Hale and a water rota, can fire in a Coldwater bar.
Scoping fixes the outbound leak; keeping every line free of names and places fixes the inbound one,
so a dockhand in the Yards running one of these says nothing untrue of any working person.

### The Null became a canonical order

Documented in [systems-ideologies.md](../systems-ideologies.md). One key deleted
(`flags.expansion`), one reciprocal `org_relations` pair with the Ascendants, and `ideology_null`
added to `OPPOSED` in [plugins/augments/index.js](../../plugins/augments/index.js) — so installing
city chrome costs Null standing, while their own cable-and-spring limbs are not `augments` rows at
all and never reach that code path.

`renounce·machine` was an empty cell, so a player who had renounced the world and leaned machine
leaned **Ascendants**. Promotion closed a hole. **Existing renounce·machine characters silently
re-lean on their next `rep`** — a correction, but a visible one.

## Not built

- **Phase 3 — jamming.** `plugins/jamming/`, `item_null_jammer`, the `jam` verb, a `JAM_CHROME`
  action, and a `SUPPRESS_CHROME` action in `plugins/augments/`. The last of those also fixes a live
  defect: the `weather.empPulse` handler suppresses only augment **soak**, because
  `stat_modifiers` are baked into the `players` columns at install by `applyStatMods`, so every stat
  bonus survives an EMP intact. `server/engine/statmods.js` is the substrate — no new engine work.
  Region-ambient jamming was **rejected**: an unannounced stat cliff over 4,836 uncounterable tiles
  spends the faction's signature move on ambience.
- **The `flags.emp_shadow` consumer.** Stamped on every tile, read by nothing. The handler must gate
  on the **flag**, never on `region_id` — engine and plugin code must not name content.
  `forceGridBlackout` needs no gate at all: these tiles are already permanently orphan-offline, so
  the region is immune by construction.
- **Phase 4 — the confrontation.** A project, not a phase. The Deep Data Center stays far north-east
  and the Null are far south-west, so **crossing the entire world is the cost of the endgame**. The
  **Architect must not speak** ([story.md](../story.md), [systems-corps.md](../systems-corps.md) —
  indifferent infrastructure, never a quest-giver); the assault is narrated by the Null and by
  machinery. The Curtain's existing **Invader test** (`renounce`-aligned or wanted) flags them with
  no new code. **Decide before starting whether the assault is repeatable, instanced, or one-way** —
  it would be the first thing in this game with a conclusion.
- **Analog bionics as a purchasable ladder.** Thematically inevitable, mechanically a second augment
  system. Ockley has four on a shelf and sells none of them.
- **The Studio painting pass**, for this region and the two before it.
- **A Scarletwastes overland crossing.** Filed separately: `VOIDS` has no
  `region_scarletwastes` key in either direction, so despite its own doc describing a roadhead the
  only way in is flying to The Strip. Deadwater does not repeat the omission.

## The ground, and the heat under it (2026-08-12)

The region shipped as one flat sheet of `ash` with a graded gravel platform in the middle of it, and
that split was doing real work: **the ground changes underfoot exactly where somebody started looking
after it.** It is kept whole. `LANDFORM_OFF` is the one place the exemptions are written down, and
nothing below paints inside the works, on a road, on the dam or on an authored set-piece.

Everything outside it is now volcanic country, and **the reason is the water**. A reservoir this
still, in a basin this dead, held at a temperature nothing seasonal explains, has something under it.
So the ground says so.

| family | terrain | tiles | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ash` | `ash` | 2042 | the original country, still what you cross most of |
| `lava` | `basalt` | 789 | frozen flows, black and sharp |
| `snags` | `deadwood` | 562 | stands that died standing, ringed around the water |
| `flow` | `plateau` | 435 | the top of an old flow, walkable |
| `cliff` | `cliff` | 307 | the flow FRONT, where it stopped. **Impassable** |
| `sinter` | `sinter` | 180 | the mineral apron the spring lays down |
| `spring` | `hotspring` | 202 | the reservoir and the tailrace |
| `ramp` | `ramp` | 76 | the breaches in the front, and the only ways up |

The apron is keyed off **distance from the lake**, not off noise, because that is what it physically
is: the water put it there, so it rings the water and reaches no further than the water has reached.
The dead stands take the ground just outside it — close enough that whatever comes up with the water
got them, far enough that they had grown first.

**The heat is never explained.** It is stated the way everything else here is stated, as maintenance:
a thermometer on a worn cord, somebody logging the temperature twice a day, nobody wondering aloud.
The one ambient beat that used to say `cold` is the only line the change cost. The name still works —
Deadwater is the flat water in a wake, and warm water lies flatter than cold.

**Checked on every build:** every plateau tile is sealed by cliff or ramp (zero leaks), and a flood
fill from the Roadhead still reaches the Tally, the eastern ruts, the gallery through the dam and the
head of the water. Roads are exempt from the landform pass, so they can never be walled off.
