# Findings: small buildings against the clone facility (2026-10-04)

Every small building model graded against the Coldwater Clone Facility, the bar set in
[building-styles.md §4.5](../reference/building-styles.md#45-the-clone-facility-is-the-bar-for-a-small-building).
"Small" means four floors or fewer on every tile the model draws, which is what the clone has.
That's 192 models over 347 tiles, after leaving out the clone itself and eight entries whose arm
never runs (see [Entries the grade skipped](#entries-the-grade-skipped)).

## Status (2026-10-05): first pass applied

The table below is the grade as it stood on 2026-10-04 and hasn't been re-scored. Fixed since:

  which is a design call: the three Reach roofs, Corner the Market's name (and five other type

  lost its second name board and gained the door its canopy had suppressed.

## How it was graded

- **Which model draws each tile.** Every `map_world` tile with a `building_name` or
  `building_type`, resolved the way `modelFor` does it (the named model first, then the type),
  with floors from `floorsFor`.
- **What was measured off each model**: the captured mesh (faces, trim faces, palettes), trim as a
  share of each elevation's wall area (the `models:elevation` measure), how much changes between
  the day and night frames, and the arm's ⚠ count.
- **What was read.** Six readers each read the clone's arm first, then every arm in one file (or
  every authored JSON), and scored eight items from §4.5: 0 is missing, 1 is partial, 2 is at the
  clone's level on that point. The items are story, built parts, door, night, name, materials,
  sides and notes.
- **Movement isn't scored.** §4.5 makes it the exception, so a still building loses nothing for
  being still, and no fix below asks for anything to move.
- **Name, where the region has no signs.** The Thornwarren and Terminus carry no lettering
  (building-styles.md 3.3 and 3.4), most of Deadwater is unsigned (3.2), and the eight Old Coldwater
  trades in `SLUM_DECLINE` carry no words. An unsigned building there meets the rule and scores 2.
- **The clone scores 15 of 16.** Its back wall carries 4% trim.

A score is a reader's judgement of the code, not of a rendered picture. The items under
[Defects](#defects-checked-in-the-code) were checked against the code; a single row's score
wasn't. Line numbers are as of 2026-10-04.

## What keeps them off the bar

Six patterns account for most of the gaps. Counts are rows whose gap or fix names the pattern.

1. **No door (about 49 rows, 68 scored 0).** Three causes:
   - A glazed band or vitrine runs the full width under the canopy with no opening in it. This is
     most of the authored shops: The Cherry Pit, Second Skin and Dead Space Interiors share one
     template; Treble Maker, Layers, Voltage, Aurelia, Adequate!, Nuts to That and Dosed & Confused
     do the same.
   - A canopy or shutter in the model claims the kit's ground section, so the kit's doorway is
     never added and the model doesn't draw one either (Loafing Around, Aurelia, Voltage).
   - The mass is yawed boxes or drums only, so the kit finds no flat wall to hang a door on
     (Artist's Impression, Fixed Assets, Deposit Taken and Peppercorn in Halcyon Fields).
2. **Parts drawn inside the mass (24 rows).** A window, door, sign ground or glow sits behind the
   building's own wall, where the depth buffer hides it. old-coldwater.js has a run of these: Stuff
   It's whole frontage stops at 0.74 fh inside a shell whose face is at 0.88 fh, and the members'
   club door, the tattooist's stair window, the lending library's window band, the off-licence
   hatch and the amusements' lit back are the same mistake.
3. **Long, thin things drawn as squares (12 rows, all in outposts.js).** `draw3DBoxAt` takes its
   depth as the last argument (`fd`); left out, a box is as deep as it is wide. So a beam, rail,
   bench, rack or shelf becomes a square slab: The Bench's gantry beam, The Tally's board, The
   Thorn Gate's mask rail, The Kept's wings and The Gate House's barrier.
4. **Lines that were never drawn (10 rows).** Washing hanging between two poles with no line (The
   Sleepers, The Whelping Room, The Wash House), a line shaft with no shaft and a belt that stops in
   mid-air (The Turbine Hall, The Forge), and a hoist block hanging from nothing (The Bench).
5. **The kit isn't declined where the arm says the wall is blind (16 rows).** The arm describes a
   blind wall and the kit hangs glazing or a shopfront on it anyway: the bookmaker, the institute,
   the land office, the fishmonger, The Reckoning and The Quiet Trade.
6. **Authored lights that burn at noon (6 rows).** The authored `glowPool` adornment
   ([windshield.js:52647](../../client/game/js/panels/windshield.js#L52647)) has no night option,
   so a lamp designed as a building's night read is lit all day: Cash & Carrion's three balls, Unit
   9 Marrow Street's security lamp, Compound Interest's floodlights, the Coldwater Sentinel's
   lantern, Unit 4 Marrow Street and Salvage Rites. One night option in the format fixes all six.

## Defects (checked in the code)

These are wrong, not just short of the bar.

- **Corner the Market is lettered BODEGA VU.** The `bodega` arm hardcodes the label
  ([downtown.js:3312](../../client/game/js/panels/glass/models/downtown.js#L3312)), and Corner the
  Market is the only building it still draws now that Bodega Vu has an authored model.
- **Three Reach buildings have lost their shingle roofs.** In
  [the-reach.js](../../client/game/js/panels/glass/models/the-reach.js#L95) on lines 95, 239 and
  307, the `draw3DBoxAt` call for the roof is joined onto the end of a comment line, so it never
  runs: The Coyote's Rest, The Dry Goods and The Quiet Trade. They've been joined since the commit
  that built the Reach (d32b67d9c, 2026-08-20), so these roofs have never drawn.
- **The Halcyon Fields transit halt's board never lights.** It's baked with the lit flag at 0
  ([halcyon-fields.js:215](../../client/game/js/panels/glass/models/halcyon-fields.js#L215)).
- **A buttress stands in the Waking Hall's doorway.** The piers meant for the flanks are placed at
  `F(lx * fh, t * fh * 1.06)`, which is the front and back walls, so the middle front pier lands on
  the double doors
  ([outposts.js:189](../../client/game/js/panels/glass/models/outposts.js#L189)).
- **Just My Type and Wrap Party have their frontage on the back wall.** Every frontage part is at
  negative `cy`. They're the only two authored models laid out that way.
- **Rod Almighty's and The Acid Test's doors can't be seen.** Each shutter is centred at
  `z: 0.01`, so it sits below the top of the plinth and behind its face.
- **Every hangar arm draws a passenger lounge and a control tower**
  ([downtown.js:2578](../../client/game/js/panels/glass/models/downtown.js#L2578)), so Threshold
  Helipad Hangar has a terminal and a tower of its own.
