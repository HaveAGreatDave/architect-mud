Question Each One Answers

To John Akerson, fellow Architect engineer — with haikus, because it was that kind of week

2026-08-13  ·  branch main  ·  mutations · augments · psionics · mastery · nullcraft

Five answers, one asked: what will you let them do to the body you have? — composed after realising the four orders had four philosophies and, until this month, exactly one shared mechanic between them: nothing

John. The factions have had opinions since we wrote them and mechanics since about a fortnight ago. This memo is the tour: mutations (the Wildblood's flesh), augments (the Ascendants' chrome), psionics (the Exodus's deniable impossibility), mastery (the Long Watch's stubborn nothing), and nullcraft (the Null's refusal to build anything at all). Five separate systems, one shared spine, and — the part I'm actually proud of — almost no new state. Most of this is old substrate finally being asked the right question.

The map: which order answers what

Every discipline is an answer to the same question — you are outmatched; what do you change? — and the answers deliberately do not overlap. This table is the whole design on one page.

OrderDisciplineThe moveThe price

The elegance, such as it is: mastery's purity cap is what makes the other four cost something. Chrome and mutation are no longer free upside, because there is one build in the game that they lock you out of — and it is the build that looks like nothing at all.

I. Mutations — the flesh answers

Rads wrote one number once, and never wrote again. All the rest derives. — on `expression`, the 1–100 scalar rolled at grant, and the several months during which we instead baked stat modifiers straight into players.stat_* and then tried to un-bake them by reversing the arithmetic

Status: Phases 1–4 built. Radiation half, Wildblood half, the authoring seam, and the cost of turning. Three rules carry it, and each one is a thing we got wrong first.

1. Expression, not a boolean

Every carried mutation has a strength from 1 to 100, rolled once at grant and thereafter only ever reduced, by treatment. Soak, acuity, who can see it, what a clinic charges to name it — all of it derives from that one number. A mutation is never "on"; it is a magnitude.

Radiation cannot roll above 84. That ceiling is deliberate and load-bearing: a legendary rad mutation you could pick up by standing in a hot room would undercut the entire reason to walk south and find the Wildblood. Rads give you the ordinary bands. The Pool gives you the rest.

2. Nothing is baked

Contributions are derived at read time and netted by the same readers that already net gear and status effects. The old model added stat_modifiers into players.stat_* and reversed the arithmetic to remove them, which is unrecoverable the instant a mutation can be partly treated. hp_max is the single exception, because it's a persisted column read from dozens of sites.

The migration invariant

scaleByExpression guarantees that a mutation at expression 100 reproduces its authored value exactly. That identity is why the move off baked stats was net-zero for every existing character, and regress asserts it. The same trick appears in augments as calibration defaulting to 100 — same reason, same proof.

3. An unread effect key is a build failure

We authored an effects object on every mutation and then read it from literally nothing for months. So the vocabulary is now a registry: an unregistered key fails the suite, and regress asserts the unconsumed list is empty. Adding a key without a reader needs a comment saying why, plus the cap raised in the same commit.

Visibility, and the thing the fee actually buys

Concealment is generic — a coat hides a torso mutation because it covers the torso, never because anyone wrote that coat into that mutation. Detection is per observer through acuitySync: bad light, bad eyes, no read. And visibly_mutated is now a write-through cache rather than a one-way latch, which means treatment restores your standing — which is most of what a physician's fee is for.

Phase 4 — the turn costs blood

Becoming costs blood. The floor is one hit point, so it can never win. — on `onset.js`, and on why the Wildblood offering you a safe room for the Quickening is a mechanic rather than a line of dialogue

Mutating is an injury. Not a notification, not a level-up. onset.js fires off the mutation.gained event that every grant path already emits: real HP that has to be mended, most of your stamina gone up front, genuine weakness while it runs, scaling with expression and worse for mutagen. Floored at 1 HP, so it can never kill you.

⚠ Regress trap

A suite that grants a mutation on the shared fake player must restore hp/stamina/statuses afterwards — or the sneak and weightbench suites go red for reasons that look nothing whatsoever like mutations.

Three medical tiers, three prices

TierWhat you getThe design note
diagnoseA name. Nothing else.Deliberately mechanically inert. An unseen mutation reads as "Something unnamed" until a physician names it — because a diagnostic that also helped would make not-knowing a penalty rather than an information state.
suppressTemporary reduction, through the one effectiveExpression chokepoint.Reaches every contribution because there is exactly one place to reach. Reports as held down, never as cured.

The social ladder (reactions.js) is socially hostile long before it is physically hostile — there is no "mutant ⇒ attack" rule anywhere and regress asserts none of its lines is violence. And it never fires in the Scarletwastes, a hard region check, because a town that recoiled from its own people would break the one thing that region exists for.

Deploy order

scripts/unbake-mutation-stats.mjs runs against prod before the new code serves traffic. Get that backwards and every stat is double-counted, on every character, silently.

II. Augments — chrome as a machine you own

A thing in a box, a thing in your hands, a thing humming in your arm. — on the discovery that an augment is three things, and that refusing to be an item first is the only reason we'd have had to build condition, quality, trade and looting a second time

Status: built. The machine-path mirror of mutations. An augment is now three things that used to be one — an authored catalog row, an ordinary inventory item while it's in your hands, and an installed record once it's in you. Which is why nothing here re-implements condition, quality, trade or looting: reusing durability.js and player_inventory got all four for free.

The schema split is the design

Every column the runtime mutates lives on player_augments. Every column an author writes lives on augments. Neither table needs an excludeColumns entry, and content:lint has nothing to trip on. That is the whole rule, and it took one afternoon and saves us the entire class of bug where a deploy overwrites a player's calibration with an authored default.

Surgery — a real roll, by a real person

Installation is a check by an NPC flagged surgeon, in five bands. You pay before the roll, exactly as the repair bench and the clinic do.

OutcomeEffect
CleanInstalled, calibration seeded high — but still under 100.
OrdinaryInstalled, seeded mid. The board is where you make it up.
RoughInstalled, seeded low, and you feel it.
BotchedInstalled, and calibration is permanently capped. It will never be right again.
margin < −10The hardware is destroyed. You still paid.

Every band seeds calibration below 100. Fresh chrome always under-performs its sheet, which is what makes tuning a thing you do rather than a thing you read about.

Calibration is scored, not passed

The tuning board reports a 0–100 score, not a win. Every other minigame family in the game is pass/fail; a boolean here would collapse calibration into a coin flip and delete the reason to be good at it. The client is bounded, not trusted — pending map, nonce, TTL, and the score is clamped server-side.

Overclock, heat, and coming apart

Run it past the line. The heat forgets on logout. The wear it left stays. — on why heat is memory-only, decayed lazily off a timestamp, and emphatically does not need a column

overclock_max is one authored field that is the entire mechanical statement of the licensed-vs-back-alley split — and it later did double duty as nullcraft's security derivation, which is the closest thing to a free lunch in this codebase. Heat accrues in memory on the combat hot path via registerStrainContributor (sync by contract), and its durable residue is condition, flushed coalesced. Cooling off while logged out is correct, not a bug.

Rule 4, inverted on purpose

durability.js destroys an item at zero condition. Installed chrome does not get destroyed — it goes dead and waits for a surgeon. A thing disintegrating inside a torso is neither narratable nor actionable.

⚠ The ordering trap

Death corruption is graded — each augment rolls independently, weighted by condition. But corruption must be skipped whenever the death was claimed, or it deletes the exact rows a cortical restore is about to write back. Check that before touching any death path.

And the authoring gate I like most: an overclockable augment with no failure_messages fails the build. "Bionic malfunction." is precisely what that guards against — a failing arm should say something a person wrote.

III. Psionics — the deniable discipline

Say what it is and it stops. So the mug just moves. Nobody saw why. — on Codex XIV, which calls psionics "the Basin's favourite joke", and on Terminus's ruling that stating a thing kills it

Status: Phases 1–2 built — psychometry, telekinesis, aegis, ergokinesis, the initiation. This is the fifth answer, and the one whose entire design problem is that describing it ruins it. The ideology row promised it in prose years ago: they "still a room and move what is in it without a touch."

1. Deniability is the progression

Below Seer, no output line may claim a mechanism. Not "you sense", not "telepathy", not "your mind reaches out". The mug moves; nobody says why. At Seer the prose is finally permitted to be impossible — and the player crossing that line IS the arc.

The law lives in one function — voice() in plugins/psionics/prose.js — with a CAUSAL_WORDS ban list and a violatesLowRank() regress seam. A law spread across forty template strings is a wish, not a law.

2. The body pays

Blood out of the ears. Something in the room gave way and it was not you. — on the strain ladder, which broadcasts to the room, because deniability that nobody can witness failing is not deniability
RungBacklashBuilt from
1Nosebleed.cosmetic + the room sees it
2Blood from the ears, failing sight.existing impairment statuses
3Seizure — you go down where you stand.knockOut, the cosh's own seam, so you are killable where you lie
4Real damage.applyStrikeToPlayer, so the injury plugin's observers hang an actual wound off it

Nothing here invents a punishment. Every rung is a mechanic the game already had, pointed inward. The backlash ladder and the deniability ladder are the same ladder — a nosebleed in a bar is a statement whether you wanted to make one or not.

3. Psychometry reads the world's own exhaust

residue.js subscribes to events that already fire, into a decaying per-zone ring buffer. No other system was modified to feed it — disable the plugin and the events carry on unchanged. An impression is fragmentary and unattributed, forever.

That last word is what stops it deleting SPECTER. Cameras answer WHO. Psychometry answers WHAT HAPPENED HERE — and then sends you off to find out who.

4. You major in one art and minor in another

psi_rank is depth; psi_focus is breadth. Top abilities are focusOnly and unreachable off-major at any cost — so there is no build that both takes bodies and walks dreams. Four gates, all data (rank, focus, resonance, cost), passed to registerPsiAbility, so no handler ever re-derives one.

Resistance is derived from Cool, Brains, mastery, Static Mind and coprocessor — with deliberately no second skill to level. And PSI_CAP is VEIL_CAP's sibling: nothing ever reaches certainty.

⚠ The verb-collision trap — it cost three renames

attune and pull are engine builtins. read is a specializedAction in six plugins. And plugin commands silently beat both classes, so nothing errors — the old verb just quietly stops working. Checking plugin.json command arrays is NOT sufficient. Grep the engine and the specialized-action registry too.

The counterpart rule: reach fires fireSpecializedAction, so telekinesis always delivers verbs that already exist rather than reimplementing them. The day somebody writes a bespoke telekinetic open is the day the system starts growing a shadow copy of the whole command set.

The Purifier and the coat

The initiation is the only irreversible cost a player chooses on purpose. And the armour taboo is purely social — armour is never blocked and never penalised, no stat hit, no refusal to equip. Two reasons exist for it and only one is ever said aloud.

Still design: telepathy, precognition, biokinesis, projection and dreamwalking, compulsion. Not registered — which, given the registry's build-failure contracts, means they cannot half-exist.

IV. Mastery — the Long Watch's stubborn nothing

The dog swings again. Nothing about you has changed. You have simply watched. — on `Read`, keyed on enemy.templateId, so four hundred dogs are one row and the four hundred and first is a formality

Status: Phases 0–5 built. The swing seam, the purity cap, the stain, Read, Exploit, composure, stances, techniques and the read window.

The rule the whole system is built on

A Long Watch veteran must not look supernatural on inspection. No paper-doll entry. No glow. And — the hard part — no permanent passive whatsoever. Everything they have, they are currently doing.

Read is the mechanical identity

Here is the inversion that justifies the system: every other build in Architect gets worse over a long fight — stamina drains, condition falls, heat climbs, strain lands. Mastery gets better. Familiarity accrues per enemy.templateId, so the fight you're in is informed by every fight you've had with that thing.

A miss teaches as much as a hit. That single requirement is why Read hangs off the new sync registerSwingContributor seam rather than damage events — a damage-event model literally cannot see the swings that missed, which are the ones you learn most from.

Purity — the ceiling, and the stain

Chrome lowers the roof. Cut it out and the stain stays for three weeks after. — on the fact that without a decay window, chrome is rentable: install for the fight, extract before the training

Rank is stored raw; the purity cap applies on read. Chrome and mutation lower your ceiling — floored at 10, because chrome makes mastery impossible, not discipline. Removing them leaves a decaying stain in player_purity that fades over roughly three weeks. Without the stain the whole cap is a rental fee.

Composure, stances, techniques

Composure is the Long Watch's overclock, made of nothing but skill — runtime only, no column, no table. Techniques are armed by a verb and consumed by exactly one swing in their own direction, and they can fail. Stances are held, drain stamina on the 10s tick, and end on four explicit edges.

The read window

A reaction minigame that had to fit inside a synchronous swing resolution: there is no point inside enemyAttackPlayer at which we can await a human. So the deadline comes from the engine's own schedule — enemy.lastAttack + interval — rather than a timer we'd have to keep honest. Letting it lapse costs nothing; the Composure was spent when the window opened. Correctness is decided server-side: the client sends the chosen word, and correct never crosses the wire.

Three presentations off the Display Mode ladder: log narrates it, textgames draws a board in the area pane, visual floats a window. The tells are the puzzle — each answer has its own and no two share one, asserted in tests.

⚠ Do not gate on the social ladder

regardOf / standingGreeting exist to be SAID, never CHECKED. Nothing may gate on them. The Watch admits all four kinds — they train them, and stand next to them in a fight. The nastiest lines are the polite ones; "you can't help what you are" is worse than any threat, and it is worse precisely because it never becomes a mechanic.

V. Nullcraft — the Null's refusal

They bought a better machine. We did not. We made theirs stop working right. — on the fourth faction answer, and the only one whose implementation was mostly a promotion of code that had already been quietly shipping for months

Status: built — the substrate, the seven operations, the turn-based intrusion board, the hardware, veiling and EMP.

The rule the whole thing rests on

Nullcraft never invents a second copy of state that already exists. A surprising amount of this shipped years ago, camera-scoped and scattered: security_devices.status_flags has held {jammed, spoofed, hijacked_by, looping, blinded} since forever, getInterferenceZones already jammed zones, isWitnessed already read it. This is a promotion of that model onto bionics, drones and locks — not a new one.

Trace is RAM-authoritative, and decays at read

The trace lives in RAM. Log out and the city holds nothing against you. — which sounds like an exploit until you notice that everything durable you broke is still broken, through its own writer

The engine file owns no table. Decay happens on read, never on a tick. A logout drops everything transient — and only durable damage persists, written by the contributor's own writer: an augment's condition, a device's status. Nullcraft never writes another system's table.

Targets arrive by gather hook

tech.targets (the workspace.provider seam) gathers what's in the room, and apply() lives on the target. That's what keeps the substrate free of imports from augments and surveillance. The integration is genuinely one line in deviceStatus() and one filter in getAugments() — so every downstream reader inherits Null jamming without knowing the system exists.

Security is derived, never authored twice

overclock_max — already the entire mechanical statement of the licensed-vs-back-alley split — does double duty as the security derivation. Licensed chrome is the hard target, and nothing new was authored to make it so.

The applicability table is load-bearing. You cannot jam a hydraulic ram. Collapse that and you have one verb with six skins. The three kinds are the tactical decision — a thirty-second servo lock and a repair bill are different crimes.

The verbs

nullscan is free on purpose — it is the discoverability rung. Detail scales with skill: below Nullcraft 3, analyze names the surface and little else.

The intrusion board

The only turn-based minigame in the client, and that is the point — everything else in the game is a reflex or a timer, and the Null are the faction that gets to think. Skill buys steps and starting intel, never a lower alert cost. A better Null goes further on the same alarm budget; they don't make the alarm quieter.

Hardware, jamming, EMP, veiling

  • Intrusion strength cancels target security. Stealth buys TIME, not power — the two axes must never collapse into one stat.
  • jammer on spends a jammer_cell and runs a field for ~4 minutes.
  • emp fires the same pulse the ion storm fires, scoped to your room. One implementation, two causes.
  • veil blurs your electronic signature — capped at VEIL_CAP = 0.85, because ghosting must never make a player unarrestable.
  • The counterplay is real and manual: player_augments.custom_data.radio_off. Telemetry is not vital — turning your arm's radio off costs you convenience, not the arm.
  • powerspike is the payoff of the whole design: damage scales with how far past spec the target was already running. The Ascendant who overclocked is the one who dies of it.

Architect's own network ladder — the infrastructure tier above all this — is design only.

What they share, which is the actual work

Five disciplines, and the reason the diff isn't catastrophic is that four of them are mostly readers of substrate that already existed. The pattern repeats so consistently it's almost a house style now:

Three of the five have a build-failure contract on authoring rather than a code review convention, and I'd like the other two to grow one. It's the cheapest quality mechanism we have: the vocabulary is a registry, an unregistered key fails the suite, and nobody has to remember anything.

The four caps, and why they're all the same cap

PSI_CAP, VEIL_CAP, the radiation ceiling at 84, and calibration seeding under 100 are one idea wearing four names: no system may ever reach certainty. A ghost who cannot be arrested, a rad mutation as good as the Pool's, a psionic who never fails, chrome that needs no tuning — each of those individually deletes a reason to play something else.

What's still design

  • Psionics: telepathy, precognition, biokinesis, projection/dreamwalking, compulsion. The Gate Keeper NPC outside Terminus still needs authoring.
  • Nullcraft: Architect's network ladder — the infrastructure tier.
  • Augments: power draw, the unlicensed Promethean path, and the paperdoll UI. Deferred by design, not by accident.
  • Mutations: nothing. Every registered effect key has a reader and regress asserts the unconsumed list is empty.
Four ways to become something else. One way to stay exactly yourself. — and the fifth is the hardest to build, because it has to be mechanically real while looking, on inspection, like nothing at all

That's the tour. The bit I'd most like you to break is the purity stain window — three weeks is a number I picked because it felt long enough to make chrome a decision rather than a rental, and I have no data behind it at all. Everything else has a regress assertion behind it; that one has a vibe.
