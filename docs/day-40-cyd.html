Haiku II · Coldwater Clone Facility

Why this one is worth finishing

The floor is already built

Most projects like this spend their first year building the thing underneath the story — the rooms, the clock, the weather, the money, the consequences. Then they start writing.

This one has all of that on day forty. Whatever story goes on top of it arrives into a world that already has rent, hunger, hangovers, police who only know what they saw, four ideological orders, corporations with treasuries, and a codex that unlocks a chapter at a time because somebody explained something to you.

That's a stage with the lights already working. It's rare, and it's the hard part, and it's done.

And it isn't finished, which is the good news

There are four files that want splitting. Some systems are wide where they'd be better deep. There are unit tests that should exist. All of these are the problems of something that works — none of them are load-bearing, and none were caused by hurrying.

Forty days in, the list of things left to do is a list of improvements rather than a list of rescues. That's the part worth noticing.
