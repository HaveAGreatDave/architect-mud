HOW TO FLY

1. Ease the throttle up. Roll down the strip until the IAS passes the rotate speed.

2. Pull the yoke back gently to lift the nose. Hold a positive V/S to climb away.

3. Half or full flaps shorten the takeoff roll and slow you for landing.

4. Watch the ⚠ STALL lamp — bank hard or fly too slow and the wing lets go.
