◄ THE .MOD FORMAT ►

— RECOVERED ARTIFACT · circa the Old Networks —

Long before the AMP, in the age of humming beige machines, sound was not recorded but rebuilt. A .MOD — a "module" — carried no waveform. It carried the score itself: a handful of tiny instrument samples and the sacred instructions for replaying them across four channels, forever, identically.

Trackers, the scribes called them. They stacked notes in glowing columns and traded these modules hand to hand across the wire. A whole song, an entire mood, folded into a file smaller than a single second of modern audio. The ancients called it demoscene. We call it a miracle.

The AMP still speaks this dead tongue. Unearth modules from the great archive at modarchive.org, then feed one to the deck with the ＋ .MOD key. Your tape will play as it did a thousand years ago.
