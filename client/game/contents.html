Welcome

Thank you for playing ARCHITECT. Genuinely. This is a text game in an age that does not make many of them, and somebody deciding to sit down and read a city is the whole reason it exists.

What you will find on the surface is simple. You read where you are, you type what you want to do, and the city answers. That surface is deliberate, and you can play a long time on it alone.

Underneath it there is a great deal more, and none of it is going to jump out and introduce itself. There are trades that are only spoken about in whispers, arguments between factions you can take a side in without ever choosing from a menu, weather that will kill you, a whole aircraft under a tarpaulin, and verbs that work perfectly well despite appearing in no menu anywhere. That depth is yours to discover, at whatever pace you like. This guide covers the parts you would rather not learn the hard way, and it stops well short of spoiling the rest.

You do not have to read any of it to start. Part one is the only one that assumes you have never typed a command in your life.
