rate is the master pace — lower is slower. Broadcast's nodeHoldMs (75 ms/char + 900) is fitted to it, so if you move this far, the hold wants refitting or lines will land on top of the voice.

breath is the only noise that runs continuously, so it drives the general impression of hiss more than its level suggests — turn it first if the voice sounds noisy.

sibilance / friction split the fricatives: S Z SH ZH CH JH versus F V TH DH. If consonants go mushy, raise friction before touching presence.

creak is phrase-final vocal fry. 0 disables it.
