Fix order

  1. Close the dialogue gap. Accept root only with no option index, and clear the open frame when a conversation ends.
  2. One way to change a balance. Replace every absolute SET credits=$1 (about 64 sites) with adjustCredits, and route the gametable buy-in, stakes and leave, jail release and rent through it too. This is the largest single job left, and it stops both the duplication and the silent losses.
  3. Escape the splice name where it's stored, and escape item names on the wares board and in dealing.
  4. Guard the remaining one-row-two-winners cases with DELETE or UPDATE ... RETURNING and act only when a row came back: cooking plate, dealing, vendor safe, sellshop, jail release/escape/pardon, housing (WHERE owner_id IS NULL), bounty (compare head time with contract time), thievery (use what was actually debited), the mortgage tick (guard the debit).
  5. Finish this session's own gaps: land the GL-install fix for lazy views, make zone-stock advance its clock when full and top up with LEAST(max, qty + delta), check the row owner in pickUp and giveToPlayer, retire old remember-me tokens, and throttle or cap the command queue before enqueueing.
  6. Then the minors.
