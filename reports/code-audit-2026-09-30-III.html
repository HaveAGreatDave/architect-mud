Fix order

  1. Close the two WebSocket exploits. Accept a dialogue choice only if it is an option of the node the player is on, with the NPC in the room; clamp the sell quantity to a positive integer, as buying already does.
  2. Tie shop messages to the open shop: the NPC must be the one the player opened and in the same room, and selling must need a vendor.
  3. Tighten staff routes: the three server-wide toggles admin-only; apiUpdatePlayer revokes tokens on a role change, validates handles and stops returning password_hash; ATM GETs dev-only.
  4. Remember-me revocation: revoke the token server-side on sign-out (or store a per-token id), and don't answer a rate-limited attempt as expired.
  5. Finish the lazy and boot gaps: lightning and boat_fuel as ifLoaded, guard tv.js and the sessionStorage calls, and tell the player when a view fails to load.
  6. Batch the fishing, mining and scavenging table loads into one read and one write per action, or cache the tables.
  7. Then the minors: type-check login fields, a password minimum, single-use reset and verify tokens, generic 500s, keyboard-reachable action links, and moving the inline handlers out ahead of an enforced CSP.
