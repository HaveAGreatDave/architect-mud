# swimming

**Purpose** — water is a **stamina cost, not a wall**. Entering a water tile is allowed; staying in it is what costs you.

- Stroking between water tiles drains stamina, scaled by the **Swimming** skill and Endurance + Brawn.
- **Treading water** bleeds it slowly.
- Running dry means you **start to drown**.
- **Diving** to underwater tiles starts a breath timer that drowns you when it runs out.
- Submersion **soaks** you (wetness), and cold water pulls your core toward **hypothermia**.
- Carrying a **boat**-tagged item rides you across dry and free.

## Commands
**None.** It is entirely automatic on movement — which is the design: you do not decide to swim, you decide to go that way. Boarding boats *from the water* is handled by the flight plugin's `embark`/`disembark`.
