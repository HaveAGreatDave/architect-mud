# crafting

**Purpose** — recipe display and a timed, posture-based craft.

## Commands
- `craft` — make the thing.
- `recipes` — what you could make.

## Duration is derived, never authored
A craft's duration comes from the recipe's **difficulty**, its **skill gate** and its **ingredient bulk**. There is no per-recipe time field to set, or to get wrong.

## Interruption contract
The craft is posture-based, so **anything that stands you up interrupts it** — and **nothing is consumed until it completes**. An interrupted craft costs you time, never materials.
