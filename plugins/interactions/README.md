# interactions

**Purpose** — the immersive action layer: how you hold yourself, what your body is doing, and the small social verbs that make a room feel occupied. Posture, emotes, observation, furniture and social interaction all live here.

## Commands
**Posture:** `sit` · `stand` · `lie` · `kneel` · `lean`
**Emotes:** `stretch` · `wave` · `shrug` · `point` · `smile` · `frown` · `laugh` · `cry` · `sigh` · `nod` · `shake` · `dance` · `pace`
**Social:** `greet` · `follow`
**Observation:** `examine` · `watch` · `reflect`

## Why posture matters elsewhere
Posture is not decoration. Half the game's timed actions (crafting, fishing, mining, scavenging, butchering, the gym) are **posture-based**, which means standing up interrupts them. This plugin owns the verbs; the contract lives in [docs/systems-posture.md](../../docs/systems-posture.md).
