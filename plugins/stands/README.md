# stands

Status: built.

A seat at the game, in the park it's played in. Golden Goose Gardens (`zone_slush_stands`) sells hockey seats and the Coliseum (`zone_hughson_stands`) baseball ones.

## Verbs

- `stands`: the seat chart. The client draws the venue from above with every section where its seat is, priced; the log gets the same list.
- `stands <section>`: buys a seat (by id or a word of its name) and opens the 3-D rink or park in first person from it. Refused when no game of that sport is on.
- `stands leave`: gets up. Walking out of the room does the same.
- `stands` in a room with `flags.box_office` (the Coliseum's club level and its stands, so the ticket machine lists it): buys a ticket into that box at its section's price (₵150 for the Platinum Box, twice the Diamond Club). Rooms carrying `flags.ticketed` (the box's lounge and its seats) turn away anybody without one, through a move gate. The ticket is for today's game: it lapses when no game is on or after four hours, and it's held in RAM, so a restart loses it.
- `sit <chair>` on furniture carrying `flags.stands_section` in a stands room: a seat in that section, and standing up leaves it. The Platinum Box's armchairs; the ticket already paid for them.
- Walking into a room whose section has a `room` (Box Seats) seats you in one of its chairs and opens the suite in 3-D, as `sit` would; walking into its lounge (the Platinum Box, the room with the `stands_room` screen) opens it on the sofa, as `watch` would. No game on, it says so and does nothing.
- `watch <screen>` / `use <screen>` on furniture carrying `flags.stands_screen`: the game from the broadcast's own cameras in 3-D, with no seat under the camera. `watch` belongs to interactions (and broadcast); both ask here first through the `watch.furniture` hook. The Platinum Box's big screen.
- A section with a `room` (`{ at: [x, z], face, floor, w?, d?, h? }` in the field's feet, `at` the middle of the glass) is a box's suite: the Grandstand draws it round the seat (`client/game/js/panels/gl/booth-mesh.js`, `setRoom` in gl/ballpark.js) and you can get up and walk it, in the game pane over the room text with the seat ladder in its bar, as the Room Stage is (a seat and a big screen keep the whole window): WASD walks, Q and E turn you, F sits in the nearest chair or sofa or uses what you are beside, T puts its big screen up full size. Its own screen shows the director's shot, drawn every third frame and blitted into the screen's texture (`renderTv`). A screen with `flags.stands_room` (the suite's zone) opens the suite on the sofa. Walking is the eye in your client; the server only knows you're in the box.
- `stands fetch <thing>`: the suite's 3-D view sends it when you click its bar or buffet (or press E beside one). The suite is one room on screen and two in the world, the box and its lounge, so a thing in the other room is walked to first. Bare, it answers with a menu of what the thing keeps (`flags.restock_items`, sent as `stands_menu`), and `stands fetch <thing> <item_id>` takes one, all on the house. The smokes are a silver cigarette box on the table between the left-hand chairs (`furn_hughson_platinum_smokes`). Crossing between two rooms of the same `flags.ticketed` box keeps a suite seat; walking anywhere else gets you up. Furniture low enough to step on (chairs, sofa, stools, tables) is climbed over, not walked round. What you've taken reaches the picture through the drug block's CSS (`stands.css`). Each item on the menu carries a `kind` (`heldAs`: glass, smoke, pour, drink, eat), and taking one plays it in first person as a lit 3-D model (`client/game/js/panels/stands-hands.js` over `stands-hands-gl.js`): the cigarette and zippo ritual ends in `smoke`, a bottle in `drink`, food in `eat`, and the bar's highball glass takes pours (`mix <it> into <glass>`), a stir (`mix <glass>`) and sips (`drink <glass>`). The bar stands off the back wall; its stock is the fridge in the corner behind it, reached only from behind the bar. Staff (`isStaff`, plugins/admin) walk past a box's usher at any time, and a suite opens in 3-D with or without a ballgame (`suiteChannels`): with none on, the park through the glass is empty and the suite's screen shows the live hockey (`tvChannelId`), drawn by a rink view kept out of sight (stands.js `hockeyOnScreen`, ballpark.js `setTvImage`). With no hockey on either, the screen plays the last ballgame again: `startSportsRerun` in plugins/broadcast rebuilds that slot's graph and walks it from the top on the player's own channel, `rerun:baseball`, which only the suite's hidden grandstand view (stands.js `rerunOnScreen`) is tuned to; it holds on the last picture when the game ends, and getting up stops it. The betting desk and standings never see a rerun.
- `stands order [item_id]`: the concessions, opened by clicking the 3-D cupholder on an ordinary seat's armrest at either venue (`client/game/js/panels/stands-cupholder.js`, drawn with the hands' renderer, with a clear button laid over it for the click and the keyboard). The room's stands carry the menu (`flags.stands.menu`, `[{ item, price, group? }]`), and `concessionGroup` files each item under food, drinks, cigarettes or cigars: a drug is a smoke, and a cigar if its name says so; something that feeds you without hydrating is food; the rest are drinks; `group` overrides. Bare, it sends the menu as `stands_menu` with target `order`, `groups` and the player's credits, and the card puts each group under its own heading. With an item id it takes the price, hands one over and sends `stands_hand`, so the seat holds it in first person through the same hands as a box (`stands-hands.js`: a cigar gets the cutter first, a drinkable mixer such as cola is held as a drink). A runner brings it; nobody walks the rows. A box's suite has its own bar, so it refuses there.
- Lighters are collectable: an item with `tags.lighter` is a look for the zippo in the smoke ritual (case, trim, finish, an inlay), and every smoke handed to you (a box's menu, `stands order`, a vend) carries the best one you hold (`lighterOf`, highest `rank`). With none, the zippo is plain chrome. `item_zippo_black_gold` is the first: black lacquer with a gold deco inlay. `scripts/oneshots/give-lighter.mjs <handle>` gives one.
- `use <ticket machine>`: the same chart as `stands`, from a piece of furniture carrying `flags.ticket_machine` in the stands room. Examining the machine offers `use`.

## Content

`flags.stands` on a room: `{ sport: 'hockey' | 'baseball', sections: [{ id, name, price, eye, note, across?, rows?, pitch?, row? }] }`. `eye` is the seat's head in the 3-D view's frame, in feet: for hockey the sheet's (x 0..200 along, y 0..85 across with the near stands at y < 0, z up), for baseball the field's (home plate at the origin, +x toward third, +z to centre field, y up). The chart is laid out from the same numbers.

## How it works

A seat is a receiver. Buying one tunes the player to the channel carrying that sport's game (`liveSportsChannel` in plugins/broadcast) through the Tablet TV's per-player path (`tablet_tv.watch`), so the game is the broadcast's game. A player with the Tablet TV open on another channel loses it while seated. The two share one tuner, so when the tablet's TV goes off the seat takes it back (`seated.tuned`).

The client (`client/game/js/panels/stands.js`) registers as one more TV view (`addTvView` in tv.js) and mounts the TV sub-screen's view with `opts.seat`: `gameday-rink.js` sets `R.seat`, which `rink/camera.js` turns into a free-heading camera (`seatFrame`, `C.ax`), and `gameday-grandstand.js` wraps the director's shot in `seatShot`. Following, the eye turns to what the director looks at; free look is drag, arrows and the wheel; F switches. Only GLASS 2 turns the rink's camera; the 2-D fallback shows the broadcast view.

A ticket is for a section, not a seat. A section is a block of seats round its `eye`, the best one: `across` seats a row (7) `pitch` feet apart (2), and `rows` rows (3), each `row: [back, up]` feet behind and above the one in front (the sport's default). A buyer gets the free seat nearest the eye, so a second buyer sits beside the first; a full section is sold out. Who is where lives in the seated map in RAM.

The ticket is paid once per seat; moving section is a new ticket.

A ballpark seat gets the day's weather cells in `stands_open` (`field`, every cell, not a crop) and draws them overhead (`gl/cloudvol.js` through `gl/ballpark.js`), moving them along itself with `client/shared/wx-cells.js`, as the windshield does. The server sends a fresh copy (`stands_sky`) only when the field changes: the weather plugin's `weather.field` event on a reseed or a moved cell, and `weather.event`. Nothing rides on the per-play payload.
