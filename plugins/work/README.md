# work

**Purpose** — Steady Work: tier-two employment, a step above the job board, and XP-gated so it is something you graduate into. Two archetypes, deliberately different in shape:

## SHIFTS — at a venue
Clock in, ride a posture-tick of timed skill-check events, and keep the manager's satisfaction up or get sent home early. Wage is flat, **plus tips** — so competence pays on top of attendance.

## COURIER — across the map
Carry a **real, keepable parcel** to a destination before a deadline. Three grades: **clean** and **sketchy** off the board, **hot** from a fence. You can `crack` the seal to steal the contents — which burns the run. That choice is the whole design of the archetype.
