# ORACLE recording script, moods and effort (Parts 6 to 9)
#
# The second session, after oracle-recording-script.txt. ORACLE's moods and loudness
# levels (VOICE_MOODS and voiceEffort in client/shared/delivery.js) are taken from
# published studies of other people's voices. These lines give the same sentences
# in each mood and at each loudness in your voice, so those tables can be fitted to
# you, and so ddsp/mood-test.mjs has a human score to beat.
# 168 lines, about 20 minutes with pauses. Split it over two sittings if your voice tires;
# Part 7 and the yells in Part 6 are the hard ones.
#
# HOW TO RECORD
# - Everything in the first script's HOW TO RECORD still holds: the same room, mic and
#   distance as the first session if you can, WAV or FLAC, no noise suppression or auto gain.
# - Set the input level on the YELLS first (line 331 and the ones after it). A yell
#   that clips is useless, and a whisper that's quiet is fine. Don't touch the level after that.
# - One file per part: part6.wav, part7.wav, part8.wav, part9.wav. Same rules as before
#   for stopping partway and for retakes (the last reading of a line is the one kept).
# - This time DO act, but play it straight, as a person in that state would really say
#   the line, not a cartoon of it. If you couldn't tell the mood with your eyes shut,
#   it's too small; if it sounds like a cartoon, it's too big.
# - Don't change the WORDS to fit the mood: no added "ugh" or laugh inside a line.
#   Laughs, sighs and the rest have their own lines in Part 9.
# - Read the [bracketed] direction before the line to yourself, never aloud.

#
